# Speek

Impromptu speaking practice: the user is given something to speak about, speaks for about a minute, and gets feedback on how they spoke.

## Language

**Topic**:
The subject the user is asked to speak about.
_Avoid_: Prompt, question

**Attempt**:
One recording of the user speaking on a Topic, together with its Metrics and Coaching. Recording again on the same Topic makes a new Attempt.
_Avoid_: Session, take, speech

**Metrics**:
The objective measurements of an Attempt (WPM, Fillers, Pauses), calculated rather than judged.
_Avoid_: Stats, score

**Coaching**:
The written interpretation of an Attempt: a few strengths, a few improvements that quote the user's own words, and one thing to try next time. It never includes a score.
_Avoid_: Analysis, review

**Feedback**:
Everything the user is shown about an Attempt: its transcript with Fillers and Pauses marked, its Metrics, and its Coaching.
_Avoid_: Results, report

**Filler**:
A vocal hesitation sound with no word meaning, such as "um" or "uh".
_Avoid_: Filler word, disfluency

**Verbal tic**:
A real word or phrase used habitually as padding, such as "like", "you know" or "basically", that is a tic only in some contexts.
_Avoid_: Filler, crutch word

**Pause**:
A noticeable silence between words in an Attempt. A Filler inside the silence doesn't break it, so "and then… um… we" is one Pause.
_Avoid_: Gap, silence, hesitation
